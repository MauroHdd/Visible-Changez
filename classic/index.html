Good to Know

FAQ

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes! Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead guarantees your chair.

Do you cut all hair types?

Absolutely. Our licensed team has 20+ years of experience with every hair texture: straight, wavy, curly and coily.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. "Young Guys" under 12 are $35 (includes a $5 appointment fee). We're patient with first-timers.

Do you offer senior and military-friendly cuts?

Seniors get a classic cut for $20. Active duty and veterans: we know regulation cuts and military fades inside and out.

Do you offer mobile service?

Yes, mobile service is available. Call the shop at (850) 626-2777 to set it up.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Use the link in your Booksy confirmation text, or call the shop. Please give us a heads-up so someone else can take the slot.
